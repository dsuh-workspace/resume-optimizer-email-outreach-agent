Role: Business Analyst APX Intern – Business Development & Strategic Partnerships

Job Details
New ideas are all around us, but only a few will change the world. That’s our focus at JPL. We ask the biggest questions, then search the universe for answers—literally. Responsibilities:
The Business Development & Strategic Partnerships function at JPL supports the identification, development, and capture of work with a range of external sponsors, including commercial, civil, and international partners. This portfolio requires effective tools, data visibility, and process support to enable opportunity tracking, pipeline management, and informed strategic decision-making.

This Business Analyst APX internship is designed to support the development of data-driven tools and lightweight applications that enhance the team’s ability to manage and scale business capture activities. The participant will contribute to efforts that improve how the organization tracks opportunities, analyzes portfolio performance, and streamlines internal processes. In doing so, the participant will help translate business needs into practical, user-focused solutions—leveraging JPL’s collaborative and data platforms to build dashboards, automate workflows, and develop internal tools and sites that enable more efficient and informed decision-making across the portfolio (e.g., Microsoft Teams, Power BI, Power Automate/Power Apps, and SharePoint-based environments).

Tasks include but are not limited to:
• Working alongside team members to understand business development workflows, data needs, and opportunity lifecycle processes
• Applying classroom knowledge to design and develop dashboards, trackers, and lightweight applications that support opportunity tracking and portfolio visibility
• Gaining experience in process improvement by supporting the automation of manual workflows using appropriate tools
• Learning how to organize and structure data from multiple sources to enable consistent reporting and analysis
• Assisting in the development of internal team sites or knowledge-sharing platforms to improve information accessibility and usability
• Supporting the preparation of business development materials, including proposal summaries, pitch decks, and briefing content for internal leadership review
• Gaining exposure to portfolio analyses such as sponsor assessments, opportunity evaluations, and risk-informed decision support
• Iterating on tools and solutions based on feedback, learning how to refine products for usability and adoption.

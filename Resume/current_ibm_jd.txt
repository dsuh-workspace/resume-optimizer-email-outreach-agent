Introduction
At IBM, technology creates value when it helps organizations solve meaningful business challenges. As an Entry-Level Brand Sales Specialist, you will create demand, build trusted client relationships, and connect customers with IBM solutions across AI, data, automation, cloud, and infrastructure. This role combines consultative selling, digital engagement, customer success, and ecosystem collaboration while helping you build a foundation for a career in technology sales and client leadership.

IBM’s Sales Internship Program culture will set you up for positive impact and success, whilst ongoing development will continually advance your skills. Our sales environment is collaborative and experiential. Part of a team, you’ll be surrounded by bright minds - always willing to help- as you apply passion to work that will compel our clients to invest in IBM’s products and services.

Your role and responsibilities
• Create demand and identify new opportunities within assigned accounts, territories, and offering portfolios.
• Engage and qualify prospective clients through phone, email, social media, video outreach, events, marketing programs, and other digital selling channels.
• Develop and maintain an active pipeline by uncovering expansion opportunities, renewals, upgrades, modernization needs, and customer lifetime value opportunities.
• Participate in discovery conversations to understand customer business objectives, priorities, challenges, success criteria, and buying needs.
• Position IBM value propositions as a trusted advisor by aligning brand offerings to customer goals, industry trends, and measurable business outcomes.
• Collaborate with Technology Sellers, Technical Specialists, Customer Success Managers, Account Executives, Business Partners, Marketing, and Ecosystem teams to progress opportunities and support successful deal execution.
• Communicate IBM technology value clearly to business and technical stakeholders through presentations, proposals, account plans, and business storytelling.
• Use digital and AI-powered sales techniques, customer data, market insights, and contemporary offering knowledge to improve engagement strategies and achieve strategic brand outcomes.

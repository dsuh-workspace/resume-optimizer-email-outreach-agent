APPLYPILOT

A little setup.
Then just click Autofill.

Connect Chrome once. ApplyPilot will start itself whenever you need it.

  1. Enable the Chrome connection

    This installs a small connector for your Chrome account on this Mac. No login task or always-on background service.

  2. Load the extension

    Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, then click Load unpacked and choose this folder:

    If ApplyPilot is already there, click its reload button. If its ID differs from the one below, remove the old extension and load this folder again. Your resumes and saved application progress stay on this Mac; written answers need review again.

    Expected extension ID

  3. Open a job application

    Go to a hosted Greenhouse or Lever application. Pin ApplyPilot from Chrome’s Extensions menu, then click its icon.

    Choose or prepare your resume → Autofill this page. You review written answers and submit the application yourself.

# Job Application Follow-up (Touch 1: Day 1 Outreach)

Use this skill to draft warm, highly tailored follow-up emails after submitting a job application.

---

## 🎯 Target Recruiter & Hiring Manager Strategy

Always aim to contact **both the Hiring Manager and the Recruiter**, with **Hiring Manager outreach given highest priority**:
1. **Priority 1: Hiring Manager (Team Lead / Direct Decision Maker)**
   - *Target Titles*: Head of Product, Director of Engineering, VP of Sales, Lead Product Manager, Engineering Manager.
   - *Strategy*: Focus on **hands-on technical execution, roadmap contribution, and concrete business metrics** (e.g. saving 50 sales hours/week). Speak to team problems and product architecture.
2. **Priority 2: Recruiter (Talent Partner / Gatekeeper)**
   - *Target Titles*: Technical Recruiter, University Recruiter, Talent Acquisition Partner.
   - *Strategy*: Focus on **role qualification match, submitted application, and fast resume review**.

---

## 🔒 Mandatory Guidelines

1. **Concrete Metrics & Projects (NO GENERIC CLAIMS)**:
   - Paragraph 2 MUST cite **1 specific project or work experience with exact quantifiable metrics** from Daniel's profile that directly matches the JD.
   - Examples of verified metrics from `master_profile.json`:
     - *AI / Automation / Sales Tech*: "built an automated pipeline with Python and Gemini that saved **50 sales hours/week** qualifying leads at Autopilot Local."
     - *Machine Learning / Data Quality*: "designed evaluation benchmarks achieving **90% classification accuracy** on golden datasets."
     - *B2B Sales / Partnerships*: "negotiated revenue-share partnerships across **7+ locations reaching 5,000+ monthly users** at Aroma Vault."
     - *Outbound / Lead Gen*: "executed outbound campaigns achieving a **15% response rate across 300+ enterprise prospects** at ClayThis."
     - *Multi-Agent Systems*: "developed an autonomous multi-agent recruitment engine using Python, Google Antigravity SDK, and Gmail APIs."

2. **Punchy and Concise**:
   - Total email length: **80–120 words** (easily readable in 30 seconds).

3. **Subject Line (Option 2 Standard)**:
   - `Following up: [Role Title] — Daniel Suh`

4. **Tailored Resume Mention**:
   - Explicitly state that the custom-tailored resume PDF is attached.

5. **Signature Block**:
   ```text
   Thanks for your time,
   Daniel Suh
   linkedin.com/in/daniel-suh-994411297 | 661-476-8194
   ```

## 📧 Email Structure Examples

### 🥇 Example A: Hiring Manager Outreach (Priority 1)
```text
Hi [HM First Name],

I recently applied for the [Role Title] position on your team and wanted to reach out to you directly. I've been following [Company]'s work on [specific product/initiative], and admire how your team is approaching [technical/product problem].

With a background in Business Administration and Data Science at UC Berkeley Haas, I recently [1 specific project + exact metric matching this JD, e.g. built an automated pipeline with Python and Gemini that saved 50 sales hours/week at Autopilot Local]. I’d love the opportunity to contribute this hands-on technical execution to your team's upcoming roadmap.

I’ve attached my tailored resume here. If you have 10–15 minutes sometime in the coming weeks, I’d value the chance to hear more about your team’s technical priorities. Happy to work around your calendar!

Thanks for your time,
Daniel Suh
linkedin.com/in/daniel-suh-994411297 | 661-476-8194
```

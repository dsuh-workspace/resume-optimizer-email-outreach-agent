# Quick Bump Follow-up (Touch 2: 2-Business-Day Threaded Nudge @ 9:06 AM Pacific)

Use this skill to draft a concise, warm "nudge" reply sent in the **same email thread** 2 business days after Touch 1 (at 9:06 AM Pacific) if no response is received.

## Mandatory Guidelines

1. **Ultra-Short Threaded Nudge (20–40 words)**:
   - Must be a quick reply that sits on top of the original Touch 1 email in the same thread.
   - Acknowledges busy schedules with empathy ("totally understand things get busy").
   - Low-pressure, genuine interest in connecting ("Would still love the chance to connect if timing works on your end").

2. **Subject Line**:
   - Matches the original thread: `Following up: [Role Title] — Daniel Suh` (or `Re: Following up: [Role Title] — Daniel Suh`).

3. **Signature**:
   - Always conclude with Daniel's standard signature block:
     ```
     Thanks for your time,
     Daniel Suh
     linkedin.com/in/daniel-suh-994411297 | 661-476-8194
     ```

## Email Structure Example

**Subject:** `Following up: [Role Title] — Daniel Suh`

**Body Example:**
```text
Hi [First Name],

Following up in case my note below got buried, totally understand things get busy. Would still love the chance to connect if timing works on your end!

Thanks for your time,
Daniel Suh
linkedin.com/in/daniel-suh-994411297 | 661-476-8194
```
Only create a draft. An actual Gmail SENT message starts the clock; an unsent draft does not. Inspection errors pause processing, and a confirmed reply cancels that recipient's sequence.
